🛒 SocioMart - Project Status Report

Food Marketplace Platform | Generated: September 1, 2026

📌 Project Overview

SocioMart is a food marketplace platform where buyers can browse kitchens, place orders, and rate their experience. Sellers can manage their kitchens and products. Admins approve sellers, and Super Admins control platform settings.
